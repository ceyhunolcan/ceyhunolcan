I study whether passive signals from phones, wearables, and the environment can reliably model behavioral health over months and years, and I build the methods, audits, and models that make that hold up outside the lab.

Biomedical Engineering at Dartmouth. Research with the AIM HIGH Lab (Geisel) and the Empower Lab (Thayer).
