<br>

I study whether passive signals from phones, wearables, and the environment can reliably model behavioral health over months and years, and I build the methods, audits, and models that make that hold up outside the lab.

Biomedical Engineering at Dartmouth. Research with the AIM HIGH Lab (Geisel) and the Empower Lab (Thayer).

### Questions I'm working on

- Is smartphone missingness a stable trait, or a state that moves with symptoms week to week?
- When wearable signal quality differs across groups, how much of that bias survives into the predictions built on top of it?
- Can a model pretrained on months of unlabeled passive data transfer to a new cohort with only a handful of labels?
